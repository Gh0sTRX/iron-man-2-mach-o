# RBS1 triage, 2026-10-29

Read-only inspection of the 35,029-entry conservative candidate against the user's original Iron Man 2 1.0.0 Mach-O.

- 479 entries have target word low bits 0b10 (halfword-aligned, not ordinary ARM/Thumb function entry pointers).
- Distribution: __const 242, __gcc_except_tab 189, __data 33, __cfstring 10, __nl_symbol_ptr 5.
- 273 entries additionally look like packed successive 16-bit integers, e.g. 0x250022 = [34,37], 0x270026 = [38,39].
- These are **suspicious**, not automatically proven invalid: data pointers may be halfword aligned; exception tables can encode offsets. Do not delete them en masse.
- No new 'safe' RBS1 generated. The current file remains an experimental candidate.
- The authoritative next comparison is upstream MetalSyntax macho_prep.py output, plus section-specific validation.
- The Vita loader's fixed MACHO_SLIDE versus actual allocated memory mapping remains a separate blocker.
